2. Personal Calendars and Google User Data

  • When you connect Google Calendar, we use calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events.readonly to read your calendar list and events from the calendars you select within the meeting period. We do not create, modify or delete Google Calendar events.
  • Your browser requests events directly from Google APIs. We do not store imported events or Google access tokens on our servers, in Firebase, or in localStorage. .ics files are also read in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Imported events are used only in the date cells and event details on your own screen. Event titles and content and Google access tokens are not sent to other meeting participants, sharing messages or Google Analytics. Importing events does not automatically change your sticker votes.
  • The Service does not provide its operator with a feature for viewing your imported calendar events. Google user data is not used for advertising, sale, credit assessments, or training general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.
  • 언제봐? (Pickatime)’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Service currently does not provide a feature for transferring imported calendar data to other apps.

3. Information Shared Within a Meeting

Anyone with a meeting link can view its title, candidate period, participant count, nicknames, sticker selections and aggregated results. The link can be forwarded to others. When you use a sharing feature, the meeting URL and an invitation or voting status/leading dates are passed to the app you select. Personal calendar events are not included in sharing messages.

The operator can view operational statistics through an administrator-only page, including meeting participation and voting statistics and counts of distinct accounts. Calendar content is not included in these statistics.

5. Cookies, Browser Storage and Analytics

  • Login session cookies are configured to last for up to 30 days. They can be removed by switching accounts/signing out or deleting cookies. Sessions contain account identifiers, nicknames and other information needed to restore your login.
  • Browser storage may contain account-specific nickname and joined meeting caches, previous vote confirmation state, whether you have seen import guidance, and meeting drafts. You can remove this information by clearing site data in your browser. Clearing browser data does not automatically delete meetings or votes stored on our servers.
  • Our analytics events do not send meeting titles, nicknames, Kakao account identifiers, meeting IDs, shared URLs, calendar events or authentication tokens to GA4. Page locations are sent as category URLs without meeting-identifying query parameters. Google Signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled. Google may nevertheless receive technical information such as your IP address during communications and use cookie identifiers and similar information to process usage statistics.
  • Retention of GA4 cookies and events depends on Google’s cookie policies, the Service’s GA4 configuration and your browser settings. You can restrict cookies/tracking in your browser or use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Restricting cookies or storage may affect features such as maintaining your login.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Meetings, votes and account profiles: We retain these while providing meeting rejoining and history features. There is currently no automatic expiration. We process deletion requests within the necessary scope after verifying your identity. If separate retention is required by law, we will explain the applicable scope and period.
  • The meeting deletion button: This currently performs a logical deletion: the Service blocks access and excludes the meeting from lists and statistics. It does not immediately physically erase the original data. To request deletion of the underlying data, contact the email address below.
  • Personal calendars: Imported events are used only in the current page’s memory. The “Clear imported events” control removes the display data; imported events do not persist after a reload or page closure. After switching to another account or meeting, the previous calendar is not displayed. You can separately revoke Google authorization.
  • Support records: We retain these for as long as needed to resolve the inquiry or deletion request and delete them when that purpose is complete.
  • Electronic information designated for deletion is removed from the relevant storage system. External providers’ logs and backups may be subject to their own retention and deletion policies.

7. Your Rights and Disconnecting Google

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your information, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent. You can directly change your nickname and edit or remove your stickers in the Service. For other requests, contact the email address below. We may request the minimum information needed to verify your identity, such as the meeting URL or account verification. Do not send passwords.

You can revoke Google permissions at any time through your Google Account’s third-party connections. Clearing imported events in the Service clears the displayed data and is separate from revoking Google authorization. To also clear information already displayed in your browser, clear the imported events or reload the page.

8. Security Measures and Google User Data Protection

언제봐? (Pickatime) treats events read from Google Calendar and Google access tokens as sensitive information and uses the following measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access or disclosure.

  • Encryption in transit: The Service is delivered over HTTPS, and communications between the browser and Google authorization/Calendar APIs are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Your browser requests events directly from Google APIs. Google access tokens are passed in the Authorization header of Calendar API requests and are not included in shared URLs or analytics events.
  • Storage minimization: Imported Google events and access tokens are processed only in the current page’s browser memory. They are not persisted to our servers, Firebase database, server-side calendar backups, browser localStorage or sessionStorage. The Service therefore does not maintain long-term storage of these Google events or tokens. Retention and protection of the original events in your Google Account are governed by Google’s policies.
  • Minimum, read-only permissions: We request only read access to the calendar list and events. We retrieve events from the calendars you select within the meeting period. We do not request permission to create, modify or delete Google events.
  • Separation of personal and shared data: Imported events are displayed only on the requesting account’s own screen for the relevant meeting. The previous events are not displayed after switching accounts or meetings. Event titles/times and Google access tokens are excluded from shared voting records, administrator statistics, sharing messages and Google Analytics. Event titles are escaped and displayed as text rather than interpreted as HTML.
  • Ending use and revoking access: The app removes its token references when the import dialog closes and after a successful event fetch. You can remove references to displayed event data using “Clear imported events”; imported events are not restored after a page reload or closure. You can also revoke permissions through your Google Account’s connections page. These actions do not delete the original events stored by Google.
  • Protection of other Service information: Login uses signed HttpOnly and Secure session cookies. The server checks authorization for administrator pages and meeting deletion requests. Shared information such as meeting nicknames and sticker votes is available to people with the meeting link and has a different visibility scope from imported personal calendar data.
